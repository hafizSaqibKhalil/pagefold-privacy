Privacy Policy for Pagefold

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Pagefold ("the app"), published by SMK Stunning Apps Solution, handles information.

1. Your files stay on your device

All PDF features (images to PDF, merge, compress, page numbers) run entirely on your phone. The files and images you select are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. We do not have servers and we do not collect, store or read your documents.

The app only opens files you choose yourself through Android's Photo Picker or file picker. It does not request access to your storage, camera, contacts or location.

PDFs you create are saved in the app's private storage until you delete them or uninstall the app. You choose when to share them or save them elsewhere.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Information about your interactions with ads and the app, and diagnostic information

This information is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is handled by Google according to its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android device settings.

3. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your data. Files created by the app can be deleted inside the app (My files) or by uninstalling the app.

5. Security

Because your documents are processed only on your device and never sent to us, they are not exposed to our systems. Data collected by the ads SDK is sent to Google over encrypted connections.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
